Where is growth actually breaking down?

Traffic

Traffic

If pipeline feels thin, it usually traces back to one of two places: not enough demand is coming in through inbound, or your outbound motion isn't generating leads and conversations that go anywhere. Either way, the result is the same — a sales team with too little to work with.

  • Not enough leads are coming in (inbound)
  • We don't generate enough leads or conversation stops too soon (outbound)

Conversion

Conversion

Leads coming in is only half the equation. Without a repeatable process, results depend on who happens to be working the deal — and when conversion drops or deals start taking longer to close, it's rarely obvious why until you look under the hood.

  • We don't have a repeatable formula/system to convert leads into customers
  • Conversion has dropped or deal time has increased significantly over the last 12 months
Get Your Business Baseline Assessment
Systems

Systems

Traffic and conversion problems are often symptoms of a systems problem. If leads aren't tracked well, tools aren't pulling their weight, and automation isn't doing the heavy lifting on generation, qualification, or meetings — growth stays capped no matter how hard the team works.

  • We don't track leads (well enough)
  • Our tools don't contribute to more leads/sales
  • We don't use (automation) tools for lead generation, qualification, meetings
